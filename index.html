Hot water on the low rate

On a two-rate electricity tariff, your cylinder's element or electric boiler should only heat the water during the low-rate hours. The times are on the sticker by your meter.

Turn the dial through a day. The tank tops up in the yellow hours and you use it the rest of the time.
